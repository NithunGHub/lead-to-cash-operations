# 06 - Renewal and Expansion

The cheapest revenue is the revenue you already have. Renewals and expansion deserve the same operating rigor as new business, not a spreadsheet reviewed the week before expiry.

## Renewal timeline

| When | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| T-120 days | Renewal opportunity auto-created with contract terms, usage, and health snapshot | RevOps (data), CS (motion) |
| T-90 days | Executive sponsor identified, success plan reviewed, commercial conversation starts | CS / AM |
| T-60 days | Quote issued for renewal; expansion options attached | AM / AE |
| T-30 days | Paper process in flight or escalated; at-risk deals flagged to leadership | CS leadership |
| T-0 | Signed or explicitly extended; no silent auto-renewals as a strategy | CS |

Auto-renewal clauses are a backstop, not a motion. Customers who "renew" without a conversation churn the next cycle.

## Health signals

Renewal risk is visible months early if you look:

- **Usage:** Declining logins, seats, or API volume vs. contracted entitlement.
- **Support:** Ticket volume spikes, unresolved escalations, executive complaints.
- **Relationship:** Champion left, sponsor disengaged, no QBR in two quarters.
- **Value:** Success plan milestones missed, no measurable outcome documented.

Score account health monthly from these signals. A red account at T-120 gets a get-well plan; a red account at T-30 gets an escalation.

## Expansion plays

| Play | Trigger | Motion |
|------|---------|--------|
| Seat expansion | Usage > 80% of entitlement | Proactive quote before they hit the ceiling |
| New team / BU | Multi-threading reveals adjacent pain | Land-and-expand discovery |
| Product upsell | Feature usage patterns, support requests | Targeted demo tied to their data |
| Contract restructure | Co-term opportunity, pricing change | Deal desk structures the amendment |

Expansion pipeline belongs in the forecast with the same stage rigor as new business. "Might expand" is not pipeline.

## Churn save process

1. **Early warning:** Health score red or sponsor risk flagged → save plan opened within 48 hours.
2. **Save plan:** Named owner, root cause, commercial and product remedies, decision date.
3. **Win/loss on churn:** Every churn gets a reason code and a note. Patterns feed back to sales qualification and onboarding.
4. **Post-mortem for surprises:** A churn nobody saw coming is a health-scoring failure. Fix the signal, not the rep.

## Metrics

- Gross and net revenue retention (by segment, cohort, and CSM).
- Renewal rate on time (signed before expiry, not after).
- Expansion pipeline coverage and win rate.
- Time-to-first-value for new customers (predicts renewal 12 months out).
- Churn reason distribution.
